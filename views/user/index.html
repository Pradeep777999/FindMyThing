Important Information

Student Guidelines

Policy
Item Retention Period
All unclaimed lost items are held securely at the administrative office for a maximum of 30 days from date of submission. Items not collected within this period will be disposed of as per institutional guidelines.
Verification
Collection Process
To collect a found item, visit the Administrative Block, Room 102, with a valid photo ID and your student or faculty registration number. A manager will verify and hand over the item upon confirmation.
Confidentiality
Data Protection
All personal information submitted — including phone numbers and contact details — is strictly confidential. Data is encrypted and accessible only to authorized administrative personnel in accordance with MITS privacy policy.
Tip
Improve Recovery Chances
Always upload a clear photograph and provide a detailed description — including brand, color, distinguishing marks, and the exact location where the item was lost or found. Early reporting is key.
